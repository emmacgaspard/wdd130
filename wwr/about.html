Dry Oar! Let's have fun!

At Dry Oar, we turn exciting river journeys into unforgettable experiences. As a family-owned rafting company, we love sharing the beauty of nature through thrilling white-water adventures. Our knowledgeable guides work hard to ensure every guest enjoys a fun, memorable, and safe journey, whether it's their first rafting trip or their next great adventure.

History

Dry Oar began its journey on the river in 1995, and for more than 30 years, we have been creating unforgettable rafting experiences. As a family-owned and operated business, we take pride in offering exceptional adventures for every guest. Our experienced team of guides is passionate about sharing their knowledge, ensuring that every trip is enjoyable, memorable, and safe.
